# What blind and low-vision people say about navigation apps and voice assistants

For Milo, 2026-09-27. First-hand voices, 2019–2026 unless marked. About 350 items from Living Blindfully / Mosen At Large transcripts, App Store and Play reviews (US, UK, CA, AU, IE, IT), Mastodon, Hacker News, UICI's *Giornale*, Orbolandia and Blind Android Users.

**Gaps:**
- **No Reddit** (it blocks this crawler).
- **AppleVis, NFB, Blind Abilities and Perkins unreadable** (bot walls).
- Double Tap and Hadley are audio-only.
- **Italian first-person evidence is thin.**

Store links open the review page. Signal = frequency / intensity.

## 1. GPS accuracy, wrong side, early "arrived" (often / high)
- "One marker was still said on the wrong side of the street." ([Mastodon](https://mindly.social/@blind5sparrow/111388153087171076), 2023-11-10)
- With the phone in his pocket, the app says "a street intersection is ahead of me when it is actually behind me." ([Mosen At Large 159](https://mosen.org/malp0159transcript/), 2021-11-28)

**Milo:**
- Say how confident it is ("within 15 m, side unknown").
- Only say "arrived" when confidence and side of street are known.
- Take heading from movement, not a pocketed compass.
- Add a final-approach mode for the last metres to the door, which remain unsolved ([Mosen, 2024-07-03](https://www.livingblindfully.com/lb0289transcript/)).

## 2. Turn timing (sometimes / medium; thin)
- Soundscape "announces… the upcoming junction at a sensible distance… Other apps… announce things half a mile after you've passed." ([UK App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6459021379?see-all=reviews), 2023-09-05)
- "50 m is too far [for an] upcoming intersection." (Lazarillo, [US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1139331874?see-all=reviews), 2019-01-19)

**Milo:** speed-adaptive, user-adjustable lead distance, "prepare / turn now", and a confirmation after the turn.

## 3. Too much talk, or missing information (often / high)
- "I don't need to hear every single bus stop on my route." (Lazarillo, [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lazarillo&hl=en&gl=us), 2020-12-20)
- "It… tells me about all the restaurants nearby and interjects directions in the middle." ([US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1139331874?see-all=reviews), 2026-09-24)
- "It never tells me where to turn or… if I should cross the road." ([UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1139331874?see-all=reviews), 2024-05-08)

**Milo:** verbosity presets; instructions outrank POIs; a street name in every instruction; POIs on request.

## 4. Holding the phone with a cane or dog (often / high)
- "You've already lost a hand with either a white cane or a guide dog harness… it's like you need 10 hands." (Vicky, [Living Blindfully 293](https://www.livingblindfully.com/lb0293transcript/), 2024-07-31)
- "I have to stop because it's difficult… to operate the touchscreen while I'm walking." ([Mosen At Large 122](https://www.livingblindfully.com/malp122transcript/), 2021-05-17)

**Milo:** on the street, everything works with the phone locked in a pocket.

## 5. Hands-free control (sometimes / high)
- "I will have to pull the iPhone out whenever I need to activate the buttons." (VoiceVista, Watch broken, [CA](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/id6450388413?see-all=reviews), 2024-05-07)
- "Urge update con supporto a shortcut di siri" ("urgently need Siri Shortcuts support"). (Ariadne GPS, [IT](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/id441063072?see-all=reviews), 2018-09-19, pre-2019)

**Milo:**
- Headset buttons on [Soundscape's mapping](https://github.com/Scottish-Tech-Army/Soundscape-Android/issues/141).
- Siri/App Intents and Android shortcuts.
- Watch wrist taps, which users like ([2021-06-09](https://www.livingblindfully.com/malp0129transcript/)).
- Shake gestures opt-in only, because walking can trigger them ([2025-12-25](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6450388413?see-all=reviews)).

## 6. Battery (sometimes / medium)
- "Google Maps was using a whopping 31% of my battery." ([Mosen, 2021-09-15](https://www.livingblindfully.com/malp0148transcript/))
- "Non mi piace… che il mio cellulare verrà scaricato in mezzo alla strada" ("I don't want my phone dying mid-street"). (WeWALK, [IT](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/id1344297911?see-all=reviews), 2026-03-04)

**Milo:** adaptive GPS rate, a sleep mode (praised in VoiceVista), no camera left on.

## 7. Stopping in the background or when locked (sometimes / medium)
- He carries a second, Android watch to avoid GPS apps being "closed in the background." (Elijah, [Living Blindfully 246](https://www.livingblindfully.com/lb0246transcript/), 2023-09-06)
- "The app stops working whenever Wi-Fi signal gets low." (VoiceVista, [UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6450388413?see-all=reviews), 2024-10-29)

**Milo:**
- Android foreground service, with help for Samsung-style battery killers.
- Survive phone calls.
- Cache routes offline.

## 8. TalkBack/VoiceOver and audio conflicts (sometimes / high)
- "When the talkback is on… the application's voice is turned off." (NaviLens, [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.neosistec.NaviLens&hl=en&gl=us), 2023-08-05)
- Soundscape for Android "crashed my TTS so hard I had to do a forced restart." ([Mastodon](https://dragonscave.space/@dhamlinmusic/114982343400964493), 2025-08-06)
- Announcements "clipped… because the Bluetooth headphones go to sleep." (Mike May, [Living Blindfully 290](https://www.livingblindfully.com/lb0290transcript/), 2024-07-10)

**Milo:**
- Proper audio focus and ducking.
- A short lead-in silence so Bluetooth headphones wake up.
- Never talk over the screen reader.
- An obvious "stop talking" control.
- Test on Samsung TTS.

## 9. Rigid voice commands vs. natural speech (rare / high; thin)
- "I say one thing, it interprets it as something else. As a native English speaker…" This user then gave up on the app's navigation. (WeWALK, [UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1344297911?see-all=reviews), 2026-06-30)
- "I can just tell Siri I want to walk to the Apple Store and suddenly I have all the directions." (Judy Dixon, [Mosen At Large 129](https://www.livingblindfully.com/malp0129transcript/), 2021-06-09)

**Milo:** tolerate accents and noise, read the destination back, and always offer a non-voice path.

## 10. Intersections and crosswalks (often / high)
- "Other apps… can tell me on what side of the street an upcoming street is on… I would love to see this." (BlindSquare, [US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id500557255?see-all=reviews), 2021-04-16)
- In Turin, audible signals cover "oltre 80 attraversamenti, su 600 incroci" ("80-odd crossings out of 600 junctions"). ([Sergio Prelato, UICI](https://giornale.uici.it/duemila-piu-venti-uguale-cento-di-sergio-prelato/), 2020-08-10)
- Gorizia has "solo tre semafori sonori, che nemmeno funzionano" ("only three audible signals, and they don't even work"). ([UICI](https://giornale.uici.it/u-i-c-i-gorizia-la-tecnologia-li-aiuta-la-citta-non-e-ancora-amica-dei-ciechi/), 2022-10-21)

**Milo:** for each crossing, say:
- whether it has lights
- whether it has an audible signal or push button, and where the button is
- whether there is a refuge island
- which side to cross on

Let users add notes.

## 11. Trust and safety (often / high)
- "I have to remind myself not to depend fully on it, but it is a confidence boost." (Wes, once hit by a car, on Oko, [Living Blindfully 237](https://www.livingblindfully.com/lb0237transcript/), 2023-07-05)
- "Yet another app for the blind with questionable reliability." ([Mastodon](https://mstdn.ca/@JadziaAdrienne/112712553975405543), 2024-07-01)
- Audio aids "tend to impede your actual hearing." ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=35676683), 2023-04-23)

**Milo:** an addition to the cane or dog, never a replacement; honest about uncertainty; short prompts; clear data sources.

## 12. Cost and subscriptions (sometimes / high)
- "I cannot believe this needs an active paid subscription for the cane to work." (WeWALK, [AU](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/id1344297911?see-all=reviews), 2026-03-14)
- "Free as long as I agree to hand over all the data I generate." (Lazarillo, forced login, [US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1139331874?see-all=reviews), 2026-06-29)

**Milo:** free, no account, no safety feature behind a paywall.

## 13. Soundscape shutdown and forks (often / high)
- "I would not go out without soundscape. It creates a visual map in my head." ([UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6449701760?see-all=reviews), 2023-10-07)
- After the fork, Siri couldn't tell the old and new apps apart (same name), and there was "no way to migrate saved locations." (same page, 2023-09-01)
- "È assolutamente l'app migliore per la mobilità dei non vedenti" ("absolutely the best mobility app for blind people"), about VoiceVista. ([IT](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/id6450388413?see-all=reviews), 2024-05-14)

Blind Android Users ranked Soundscape for Android the top GPS app of 2025 ([2026-01-03](https://www.blindandroidusers.com/podcast/episode-265-best-apps-of-2025-part-2/)). AppleVis thread titles show confusion between the three forks.

**Milo:** reuse Soundscape's vocabulary (My Location, Around Me, markers, beacons); import GPX files and markers; stay open source.

## 14. Google Maps and Apple Maps (sometimes / medium)
- People combine apps: "maps just through Siri… then… BlindSquare to point out landmarks." (Michael Feir, [2022-06-07](https://www.livingblindfully.com/malp0182transcript/))
- Google Maps is "ottimo per i non vedenti che usano il VoiceOver" ("excellent for blind VoiceOver users"). ([Orbolandia](https://www.orbolandia.it/post/orbolandia-google-maps-un-navigatore-pedonale-2022-per-non-vedenti-che-usano-il-voiceover), 2022-08-15)
- Detailed voice guidance depends on region: "Moved to Australia which google maps doesn't have detailed voice guidance." ([Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lazarillo&hl=en&gl=us), 2023-07-17)
- **Italian availability is unverified, and first-hand reviews of detailed voice guidance are thin.**

**Milo:** mainstream-quality routing and blindness-grade context in one app.

## 15. Abandon vs. love
**People abandon apps over:**
- crashes: "oggi ho deciso di disinstallarti… crashi al primo secondo" ("today I decided to uninstall you… you crash in the first second") ([Ariadne, IT](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/id441063072?see-all=reviews), 2020-07-11)
- VoiceOver breaking after an update ([BlindSquare, 2026-09-19](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id500557255?see-all=reviews))
- developers going silent
- paywalls
- rerouting that "just tries to take you back to the original route" ([WeWALK, UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1344297911?see-all=reviews), 2025-06-06)

**People love apps for:**
- a developer "continually listening to and acting upon feedback" ([VoiceVista, AU](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/id6450388413?see-all=reviews), 2023-11-12)
- spatial audio
- independence: "finalmente non siamo più soli" ("at last we are no longer alone") ([IT](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/id6450388413?see-all=reviews), 2024-03-01)

## Other recurring themes
- **Pre-trip exploration** (supports Milo's plan):
  - "explore a location without actually visiting it" ([UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6459021379?see-all=reviews), 2023-09-07)
  - "simulate a route that starts at a point other than my current location" ([US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6450388413?see-all=reviews), 2026-03-07)
- **Custom routes, waypoints and rerouting.**
- **Outdated points of interest.**
- **Learnability:** "a button that says callout! This should really be 'where am I'" ([UK](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6450388413?see-all=reviews), 2023-07-03)
- **Italian localization:**
  - Lazarillo's Italian translation is poor, and VoiceOver reads only part of the screen ([IT](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/id1139331874?see-all=reviews), 2019-09-04).
  - VoiceVista gives lots of detail but not which Italian town you are in (2024-05-10).
- **Italy:**
  - UICI's standard is LETIsmart, a beacon-based talking cane that needs no smartphone ([2021](https://giornale.uici.it/arriva-il-bastone-per-ciechi-smart/)).
  - Apps can't replace on-board announcements for older people ([UICI Torino, 2021](https://giornale.uici.it/torino-mezzi-pubblici-e-disabilita-visiva/)).

## Top 10 needs and pain points (ranked)
1. Trustworthy position and heading, honest about uncertainty; correct side of the street; reliable arrival and final approach.
2. Crossing information: where, which side, lights, audible signal, button. Critical in Italy.
3. Well-timed, filtered speech with adjustable verbosity; the next instruction never buried.
4. Works entirely from a pocket with the screen locked.
5. Hands-free control: headset buttons, watch haptics, shortcuts, voice.
6. Reliable in the background, light on battery, and clean alongside TalkBack/VoiceOver, calls and Bluetooth.
7. No accessibility regressions; a responsive developer.
8. Rerouting, custom routes and markers, and rehearsing a route before the trip.
9. Free and lasting: no paywalled safety, no forced accounts, open source.
10. Natural voice interaction in Italian and English, good localization, and fresh map data.
